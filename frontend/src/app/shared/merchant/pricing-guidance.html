How was this calculated?

Unit contribution = discounted, tax-exclusive price − unit cost − extra variable cost − payment fees. Total contribution = units × unit contribution − tax-exclusive refunds.

Margin = unit contribution ÷ tax-exclusive discounted price. Break-even = ceiling((fixed costs + tax-exclusive refunds) ÷ unit contribution). Target-margin price solves the same equation and rounds up to four decimals; unavailable if its denominator or combined variable cost is not positive.
