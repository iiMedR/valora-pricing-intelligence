YOUR PRICING WORKSPACE

Your next pricing decision starts with evidence.

Connect your store, review its records, and understand whether the evidence supports a price recommendation. Imported data, a useful forecast, and an eligible recommendation are separate decisions.

Choose your next step

  1. Connect and sync your store

    Check authorization, import progress and retryable failures. Connection alone does not mean history is complete.

  2. Review data readiness

    Find missing costs, stale inventory and incomplete evidence, then follow links to complete inputs.

  3. Review your catalog

    Inspect products, costs, limits and dated observations. Archived records retain their history.

  4. Inspect orders and refunds

    Review immutable transaction snapshots. Unknown historical cost stays unknown.

  5. Understand observed performance

    Explore recorded sales, refund-period adjustments and contribution for known-cost records.

  6. Review pricing evidence

    Inspect forecasts, price associations and reasons to withhold recommendations. No prices are applied automatically.

Looking for the seeded data? Open the synthetic demo on port4201. In Analytics, Forecasting and Pricing Recommendations, select Full 2025 to inspect its historical sales. The normal workspace uses your own records.
