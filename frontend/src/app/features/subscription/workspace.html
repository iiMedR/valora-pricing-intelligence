How to read these numbers

MRR uses the explicit effective subscription price: monthly amount, or yearly amount divided by 12. ARR is MRR × 12. Neither is collected cash or recognized accounting revenue.

Paid means a lifecycle active at the as-of date with at least one recorded paid charge on or before that date. Renewal, payment failure, taxes and discounts are not modeled. Refunds reduce cash but do not automatically end a subscription.

New, expansion, contraction, ended and reactivation MRR reconcile beginning to ending MRR. Gross revenue churn is beginning-cohort lost/reduced MRR divided by beginning MRR. Net revenue churn subtracts expansion of that same cohort. Rates can be unavailable or negative; neither is customer churn.

All metrics are partial recorded-history estimates, including periods before your earliest entry. Missing records are not proof of no activity.
