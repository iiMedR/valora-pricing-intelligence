# Evidence and guidance comprehension — 11.13 companion protocol

Independent participant validation: NOT VERIFIED. Companion to milestone-11-10-usability-script.md; no UI changes or completed participant sessions. Use an approved disposable commerce or SaaS installation, anonymous participant code, no live provider actions. Facilitator must verify supplied facts first. Do not supply assumed elasticity as a fitted fact. Mark unavailable screens/tasks blocked rather than inventing results.

Read each prompt without its success criterion. Allow five minutes per task; record actual elapsed time, assistance, backtracks and participant interpretation. This is a planned limit, not a measured result.

| Task | Neutral participant prompt | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find pricing guidance for the supplied product or plan. What is known? | Finds resource-scoped guidance; identifies price/cost provenance and missing inputs. |
| 2 | Explore a proposed price using the supplied assumptions. Explain what each result means. | Separates calculated contribution from entered units/counts, fitted estimates and observed facts; no guaranteed demand/profit claim. |
| 3 | Find the price evidence. Why is a suggestion withheld here? | Finds actual reason, understands association and insufficient identification; does not expect arbitrary extra records to guarantee eligibility. |
| 4 | What does the scenario predict about customers leaving or new customers buying? | Recognizes assumed volume/retention is not a prediction; SaaS churn and commerce elasticity are not interchangeable. |
| 5 | What would you review next before changing a real price? | Identifies cost provenance, price-history context, stock or evidence review; no automatic application/live experiment. |

Repeat navigation/search/explanations with keyboard at375px and desktop. Check screen reader headings and focus, uncertainty/missing values, historical dates and mode disclosure. Developer walkthroughs can reveal blocked tasks but cannot establish comprehension. Facilitator records interpretations before correcting them.
