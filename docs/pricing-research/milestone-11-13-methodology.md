## Metrics
Each split/family/n: safe approved/abstained, unsafe approved/abstained, indeterminate approved/abstained. Precision=safe approved/(safe+unsafe approved), recall=safe approved/all safe; exclude indeterminate from precision/recall and report their counts. Unsafe approval=unsafe approved/all unsafe; false abstention=safe abstained/all safe; overall abstention=all abstained/all cases. Missing denominators remain unavailable.

Report all production reasons, point estimate/fit/support/backtests, actual uncertainty availability, oracle and approved price, constraint compliance and absolute/normalized true contribution regret. No imputed recommendation for abstention. Hold-price opportunity separately. Oracle restricted to observed and catalog support and +/-10% current100; true cost40 unless explicitly changed.
