# Demand estimation and forecasting — model contract

Milestone 10.5 supersedes the elasticity estimator below with `weekday-season-trend-qr-v2`, fuller weekday/trend controls, retained legacy confounding gates and additive `identification` metadata. Forecast baseline and observed-data selection remain unchanged. Scientific intervals remain unavailable. See [recommendation-quality.md](recommendation-quality.md) for the current specification and old/new benchmark.

Milestone 06 is read-only observational reporting, not price optimization. UTC ranges are `[startDate,endDate)`, 28–731 days, end no later than tomorrow UTC. Forecast starts at the exclusive end (a historical end is an explicit retrospective as-of forecast). Event timestamps, not ingestion audit timestamps, determine retrospective availability: these are event-time backtests, not a simulation of late-arriving imports.

Completed orders are dated by immutable placement. Daily gross units sum all completed lines for the variant. Returns/financial refunds do not become negative demand; returned units are separately summed on refund date. Costs are not predictors. Archived variants retain historical analysis; future forecasts are suppressed for currently archived variants/products because historical archival dates are unavailable.

The daily transaction price is `sum(line_net)/sum(quantity)`, weighted by gross units, after allocated discounts. BigDecimal remains in SQL/price DTOs until the statistical boundary. No-sale days use only a VERIFIED_CHANGE listed-price period effective at UTC day start. Snapshots/inferred prices do not establish continuous prices. Missing days are calendar rows, with zero gross recorded sales but UNKNOWN availability; they are not proof of zero demand. Listed periods and inventory are queried by event time, without future-price backfill or mutable catalog price fallback.

Availability uses the latest observation at/before day start, no older than 24 hours, excluding INFERRED. Zero stock = STOCKOUT. Positive stock = IN_STOCK, except units >= available quantity is conservatively marked CENSORED (possible sell-through). Missing/stale/inferred = UNKNOWN. Even IN_STOCK only establishes a beginning-of-day observation, not continuous availability. Censored and unknown days are excluded from elasticity and baseline training, never interpreted as latent-demand labels.

Typed configurable `app.forecasting` settings centralize minimum baseline samples (28), lookback (56 calendar days), minimum weekday samples (3), minimum elasticity samples (90), minimum distinct rounded prices (3), minimum relative spread (15%), maximum unknown/censored share (20% each), maximum price discrepancy (2%), maximum log residual RMSE (0.35), maximum absolute elasticity (5), outlier factor (4), and numerical rank tolerance (1e-8). Invalid settings fail startup. Daily transaction-versus-listed differences beyond 2% are excluded from elasticity and flagged PRICE_DISCREPANCY; no causal promotion label is invented. Zero-sale candidates are included in diagnostics; any otherwise eligible zero days make elasticity unavailable to avoid silently selecting nonzero outcomes. Positive units above four times the eligible positive median are flagged OUTLIER and excluded from elasticity and baseline; excluded counts are returned.

Elasticity method `calendar-log-ols-v1`: `ln(units) = intercept + beta ln(price/referencePrice) + weekend + sin(annual phase) + cos(annual phase) + sin(2 phase) + cos(2 phase) + error`. Two annual harmonics are modest smooth calendar controls, not generator month factors. Modified Gram–Schmidt QR with reorthogonalization checks rank; no normal-equation inverse. Reject low variation/coverage, zero selection, rank deficiency, excessive residual error, nonfinite/positive/implausible beta. Never clamp a positive estimate. Returned beta is association-based; omitted confounders, time-correlated prices and zero exclusion can invalidate causal interpretation. Calendar controls cannot identify every monthly shock. No IID confidence interval is claimed: daily aggregates are not independent customers. RMSE, price range, counts and explicit NO_CALIBRATED_INTERVAL/OBSERVATIONAL_ASSOCIATION reasons qualify every estimate.

Baseline `weekday-mean-v1`: mean eligible daily gross units by weekday over last 56 calendar days, falling back to the overall eligible mean for a weekday with fewer than three samples. Requires at least 28 eligible recent days, includes genuine in-stock zeros, and gives nonnegative expected observed units conditional on availability. Horizon is exactly 7, 14 or 30 days. Outputs are unit decimals (four places), not money; total is sum of rounded daily outputs. Assumed price is the latest known authoritative listed price within the training window, explicitly held constant. No price-response adjustment or hypothetical price endpoint is implemented. Changes from the recent price mix are flagged; elasticity does not secretly drive recommendations. No calibrated interval is available; historical dispersion alone would not establish predictive coverage, so interval fields are null with an explicit reason. This is not a recovered latent-demand estimate.

Backtests use two nonoverlapping chronological origins at end−2h and end−h, each trained only on earlier daily rows. Actuals are held-out gross observed sales, including zero/stockout/unknown days; those counts are disclosed. Compare the weekday baseline with trailing eligible mean, using MAE, WAPE (null if actual total=0), mean signed bias (predicted−actual). Return cutoffs, dates, actual/predicted totals, sample/exclusion counts and each prediction; no future realized/listed price is supplied to the predictor. Insufficient recent training yields INSUFFICIENT_DATA, not zero error. Changing future records must leave earlier-origin predictions byte-for-byte identical. Error against stock-constrained sales is not latent-demand error.

## APIs and limits

All GETs under `/api/v1/forecasting`, required ISO `startDate`, `endDate`. Existing safe 400/404 error conventions; currency mismatches refused. Forecasting GETs do not persist models or evaluations. The separate Milestone 10.5 optimization audit feature uses V6 and requires an explicit authenticated POST to save an evaluation.

- `/overview?page=0&size=10&search=`: paginated current variant/product labels/status and readiness; literal case-insensitive SKU/name search. Size 1–10, page 0–100000, search ≤100; one bounded batch daily query for page IDs, never per-variant SQL calls. At most 7,310 daily rows per request. Status is a diagnostic, not an eligibility search filter.
- `/variants/{id}/readiness`: coverage, eligible/zero/censored/unknown/missing-price/discrepancy/outlier counts, price range and status/reasons.
- `/variants/{id}/elasticity`: READY with beta or BASELINE_ONLY/INSUFFICIENT_DATA with null estimate, method/version, RMSE and diagnostics.
- `/variants/{id}/demand?horizonDays=7`: identity/range, complete daily history with exclusion flags, forecast assumption, 7/14/30 daily predictions/total or unavailable state, null interval and reasons. No scenario-price input; unknown extra scenario parameter is not a supported API feature.
- `/variants/{id}/backtest?horizonDays=7`: two chronological folds or explicit insufficient states, model/naive metrics and held-out observed counts.

Statistical values are finite nullable JSON numbers rounded to six places; expected unit strings use four places. Prices are decimal strings/currency from settings. No NaN/Infinity. READY refers to guarded observational association, BASELINE_ONLY to unavailable elasticity with usable baseline, INSUFFICIENT_DATA to no usable recent baseline. Reasons are stable codes. Full 2025 is a UI preset for inspecting existing demo history, never hardcoded results. Last 365 days uses UTC; normal empty catalog returns an empty page, no forecast.

Runtime forecasting packages must never import demo generator classes or query `demo_control` truth. Test-only generator comparisons use actual deterministic scenarios, daily aggregates and documented tolerance. Synthetic seasonal/promotion/stockout results are diagnostics, not scientific validation of causal elasticity. See milestone verification for executed estimates and limitations.
